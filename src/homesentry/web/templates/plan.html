How the score works

100 minus a weight per open finding (critical 10, high 5, medium 2, low 0.5; info 0). Each device's total saturates smoothly toward 18, so one hopeless box can't sink the house on its own, but every fix on it still counts. Unidentified devices cost 1 each (max 6). Accepted risks don't count. A 90+ is an A. CVE findings come from service banners: they're leads to confirm, and computers are capped at medium because their fix is an OS update.
